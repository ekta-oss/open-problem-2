## How it works

The activity has three steps, taken from the client's `silent-newcomer-assets` package.

1. **Your first words.** The canteen scene is shown with the script's question: "A new person sits next to you and says nothing. What do you say first?" The learner writes their own opening line (3 to 35 words). An optional word bank adds words where the cursor is: Good morning, Hi, I am, Nice to meet you.
2. **Compare and reflect.** The learner sees their reply and a short feedback message. The message is built from an internal check of six points: a greeting; the greeting comes first; only one greeting; "I am" or "My name is" followed by a name; a name, friendly line or question; and words that fit the moment (meeting, not leaving). So a reply made by pasting the word-bank words in a row ("Good morning Hi I am Nice to meet you") is not marked as correct. It praises what is there and nudges for what is missing. A farewell is named as a mix-up, and there are notes on "Good morning" at mid-day and on asking a question. **The key words and the criteria are never shown to learners**; staff can find them in the separate checking-metric document. Then comes the strong example ("Hi, I am Ravi. Nice to meet you."), labelled "for comparison, not a correction". Optional voice practice has a Practice badge and a consent tick box; it is recorded in the browser only and never checked. The learner can choose "Try another reply".
3. **Take it with you.** "Where else could you introduce yourself today?" (4 to 40 words), with the same kind of feedback message, checked for a place and what you would say there.

At the end: "Practice complete", **Download my notes** (a text file) and **Delete my saved work**.

- **Not graded:** a "Practice · not graded" badge and no score. Attempts are saved in the browser as `2.2.open-problem-log`. Nothing is sent anywhere; recordings are never saved or uploaded.
- The top bar shows "About 10 minutes" and never counts down. There is no Pause button and no time limit, as in the other Module 1 pages.
- No contractions are used anywhere on screen.
